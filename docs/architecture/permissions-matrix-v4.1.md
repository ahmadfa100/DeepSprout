# Role and resource permissions

**Target:** [DeepSprout v4.1](../../DeepSprout.md) and the [4 November P0 map](p0-target-v4.1.md). The matrix states partner permission requirements. Independent Coach Program writes remain in the legacy demo. Provider entry routes show retirement. Database mapping: [persistence contract](database/persistence-contract.md).

`Own` means an authorized resource grant, never every record of that type. One human account can hold several scoped appointments. A platform account suspension denies protected access through existing sessions; revoking one appointment ends only that appointment's future access. A pending invitation grants nothing.

**Limits:** at most 135 accepted students and three active Supervisors per Initiative, protected under concurrency. Pending/queued students do not occupy accepted seats; a valid offered hold counts for its activity capacity. The suspension-seat and 24-hour offer-expiry rules are interim configurable defaults. No Coach group count or Coach-to-student ratio is approved.

**Invitation checks:** Representative issues an unguessable, expiring, revocable, email-bound Initiative Coach/Supervisor link. Recipient signs in or registers as one human, proves the matching verified email, and accepts after server revalidation. No Supervisor `Requested` state, self-request path, independent Coach/Provider grant or extra Admin approval. Wrong email, expired/reused token, cross-Institution scope, stale session and fourth concurrent Supervisor cannot grant authority.

**Migration boundary:** existing `mentor/programs/*` and `provider/*` pages, stores and tests require route/data/retention mapping. Their current visibility is not active authorization. The server must enforce each action; UI hiding and browser-local checks are demonstration aids only.

**Current server boundary:** Identity checks the active Person and security stamp. Individual routes require enabled Individual access. Community moderation and Institution verification decisions require `PlatformRole = Admin` in SQL. Initiative admissions and staff management require the active Representative of the verified owning Institution. Representatives and active Initiative Supervisors can manage group placements in their scope. Coach rosters require the exact active appointment and group assignment. Students can read their own history and current group context while their admission is active. Supervisor admissions remain future server work. New Representative grants await approved email ownership, legal and Representative evidence, and secure document handling policies.

Initiative assignments and decisions use live group placement and appointment checks. A Student can read their own authored attempts and feedback after a transfer. A restricted decision note is available to its reviewing Coach within their current group scope and to active Initiative Supervisors. The Institution report inbox contains submitted Supervisor summaries and their included Coach reports.

The live Group Garden uses the owning Institution's verified Representative membership, an active Coach or Supervisor appointment for the Initiative, or current accepted Initiative participation through Student or Individual. The shared projection exposes collective counts and contribution history without Student identities, submission content or review notes.

Initiative Session writes require verified Representative authority for the owning Institution. The Representative and active Initiative Supervisor read the roster in scope. Current accepted participants may register in an offered mode and read their own registration history. Public discovery shows published Sessions and aggregate availability. Online access is available during the Session to currently eligible confirmed online registrants.

The verified Representative assigns Session duty to an active Initiative Supervisor. Verification and corrections require that exact current duty. Participants read their own factual status and excuse. An active Initiative Supervisor recommends, and the verified Representative records the final decision after the recommendation.
