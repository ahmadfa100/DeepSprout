# 12 — Evidence, rewards, reporting and audit

Each owning module decides whether an original fact is valid and emits one `EvidenceEvent` with stable `EventPublicId`, `(SourceContext,SourceKey,SourceVersion)`, subject, provenance, kind and effective time. Source records may hold its `EvidenceEventId` as a relational link. The event envelope is not a polymorphic replacement for the original FK graph: original PracticeAttempt, RoutineCheckIn, ReviewDecision, Attendance or Completion remains the authoritative fact. The source writer and outbox insert commit together. A revised factual decision emits a new source version/invalidation; it never edits old evidence away.

| Source | Provenance | Possible use | Explicit non-use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Valid PracticeAttempt / approved assignment ReviewDecision | system-scored or Coach-reviewed, with content/review version | Corresponding one of four personal practice trees; correct Initiative or independent group contribution | No general health score; duplicate review/partnership link creates no second effect |
| RoutineCheckIn | self-reported | Bounded flower only under published policy | Arbitrary to-do ticks and repeated same-day check-ins do not farm rewards |
| SessionAttendance Present/Late | active Supervisor confirmed, method and registration | Bounded rabbit if eligible; source-based attendance report | Booking/QR display, Absent or accepted excuse grant none |
| CampCompletion | Provider/Supervisor verified under published rule | Bounded birdhouse | Check-in alone or purchase grants none |
| Initiative/Program completion | published completion evaluation | Shared history/report classification; eligible collective milestone | No fifth Personal tree or decoration |
| Assessment/Shield usage | private test/browser observation | Source-labeled Insights and Recovery recommendation inputs where valid | No automatic universal recovery score or Garden effect |

`RewardPolicyVersion` freezes eligibility/caps. `PersonalRewardEntry` and `GroupRewardEntry` have unique grant keys **without policy version** in the key, so re-evaluation under a newer rule cannot duplicate an old source effect. The worker rereads current source validity and processes correction versions in order before issuance; a correction arriving first cannot leave a stale grant without compensation. Bounded effects are checked under a Person/Garden lock or derived by ordered grant entries; the projection is rebuildable. A source invalidation writes a Reversal linked to its Grant and updates projections; restoring validity requires an explicit new decision with audit, not deleting the reversal. Policy migration must state whether old evidence is grandfathered or recomputed. Missing recent data is neutral; never derive decay from elapsed time alone.

Insights queries expose five independent domains: browser usage with coverage, plan adherence with due denominator, practice validity/levels, Lab valid comparable attempts, and offline/experience participation split by provenance. Any chart/report includes definition version, period, source watermark, exclusions and missing-data treatment. Coach→Supervisor→Representative reports derive from original task/attendance/admission records and narrow authorized fields; they cannot infer personal Lab/Shield changes from Initiative completion. `ReportSnapshot` is a cache, not a second truth.

`AuditEvent` records actor, selected grant, resource, before/after hashes or safe status summary, reason, time and correlation. Sensitive read/export/file access is audited too. Avoid raw secrets, browser domains, assessment answers or excuse bodies in audit payloads. A separate tamper-resistant log destination and retention/access policy are required in production; a SQL table alone is not tamper proof. `OutboxMessage` and `Notification` preserve delivery result separately from the committed domain fact; worker retries are idempotent by event/recipient/template keys.
