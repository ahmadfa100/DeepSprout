## Required workflow and demo contract

The target record chain is **Institution → Initiative → optional Initiative Enrollment → Session → separate Session Registration → Attendance → Completion**, with Form Submission and Competition Entry distinct; an external Provider Experience remains Provider-owned. An approved representative drafts a valid initiative, publishes it, and regular Individual Explore shows its current version. A full Individual can open details, join if required, then separately register for a session in Online/In-person/Hybrid mode. Confirmation appears in their profile/notifications and Institution Participants. An **active authorized Supervisor** records factual attendance by the method appropriate to the session mode; declared completion is evaluated separately. Institution Reports and Individual Insights show source-qualified participation, while an eligible attended session may unlock the bounded rabbit and Initiative completion adds no Personal Garden object. A canceled or suspended resource leaves history but stops new registration and communicates the state. This is required for Demo A; the current partner-only version cannot satisfy it.

The static demo can implement coherent shared local records, source IDs, state guards and visible notices. Server identity, document verification, secure staff grants, multi-user seat concurrency, reliable delivery and tamper-proof audit belong to final backend. Open details: institution vetting documents and finer staff capabilities, identified-survey repeat/retention rules, competition appeal policy, Garden thresholds, meeting-link delivery and cancellation notice channels. Structured Initiative student admission uses one Rep-or-Supervisor approval. See [permissions](permissions-matrix.md), [Individual matrix](individual-integration.md), [gaps](gaps-and-decisions.md).

## Approved target correction

The Representative may publish an Initiative before a Supervisor accepts; an active assigned Supervisor is required **before attendance is recorded**, and `Needs Supervisor` must alert/disable confirmation. There are at most **135 accepted students and three active Supervisors per Initiative**. Representative or Supervisor may approve one shared Application; only Representative rejects/removes/suspends/restores/blocks and revokes staff. Structured institutional training remains an Initiative with internal Groups, not a duplicate Program. Phase-1 surveys are identified, payments simulated, and publication must reach regular Individual Explore.
