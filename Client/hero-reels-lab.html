02 / A name for the feeling

Brain Rot.

You’ve probably heard the term.

It’s a popular way of describing the mental fatigue and sense of lost focus people associate with consuming too much low-value digital content.

A cultural term, not a medical diagnosis or evidence of brain damage. Oxford Languages named “brain rot” its Word of the Year for 2024.
