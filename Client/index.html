        From scrolling to something real

        The path every DeepSprout member walks, one step at a time.

        1. 01Name your goalLess short-video, more intentional phone use, more offline time — you choose.
        2. 02Measure your starting pointFour short checks in the Lab: attention, response control, reading depth and habits.
        3. 03Follow a four-week roadmapOne focus per week. Each week is written after you review the last.
        4. 04Swap the scroll for real thingsTimed practices, plus a small offline routine you choose and tick off yourself.
        5. 05Review, and watch it growA weekly review shapes next week. Your garden grows from what you logged.
