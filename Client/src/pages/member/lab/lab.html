The four instruments

Each one records its own result, its own version and its own date.

How to read these results

  • Four assessments, not four diagnoses. Each measures something different. There is no combined percentage and no brain-health score.
  • Practice effects are real. Repeating a task often improves the score without anything else changing. That is why formal retests open on a schedule rather than on demand.
  • Comparisons need matching versions. Two attempts are only comparable when the same instrument version and conditions were used.
  • Reassessment is free. Seeing your own progress is never placed behind a paid plan.

Who can see this

Your assessment results are private by default. Mentors, institutions and outdoor providers do not see them.
