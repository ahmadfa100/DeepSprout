    The four tasks

    Each records its own result, version and date. Time estimates include instructions and untimed answers; actual duration varies. Practice rounds are open any time and never counted.

    How to read these results

    • Four tasks, not four diagnoses. Each measures something different. There is no combined percentage and no brain-health score.
    • Practice effects are real. Repeating a task often improves the score without anything else changing. That is why official checks open on a schedule.
    • Comparisons need a real re-measurement. A comparison appears only for a task that was actually measured again. Practice rounds are never compared.
    • Re-checking is free. Seeing your own progress is never placed behind a paid plan.

    Who can see this

    Your results are private by default. Mentors, institutions and outdoor providers do not see them.
