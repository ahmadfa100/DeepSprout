Your starting point.

Four assessments, four separate results. There is no overall score here — combining a questionnaire, two timed tasks and a reading test into one number would not mean anything.

What these results are, and are not

  • They describe one attempt on one day. Tiredness, interruptions and the room you were in all moved them.
  • They are not a diagnosis and not a measure of brain health. No clinician has reviewed them.
  • The questionnaire is a self-report. It sits beside the task results, never averaged with them.
  • They are a reference point for you, so a later attempt has something to be compared against.
