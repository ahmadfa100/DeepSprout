What DeepSprout does with your answers

Your setup answers and assessment results are private by default. They are used to choose which activities your plan suggests and to let you compare your own results over time.

Mentors and institutions do not see your assessment results or browsing records. In this client preview nothing leaves your device — everything is stored locally in your browser.
